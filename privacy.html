Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

"The Margin Report Autopilot" ("the app") is a single-user automation tool that produces and publishes videos to one YouTube channel, "The Margin Report," on behalf of its sole owner and operator. This policy explains what data the app accesses through Google APIs, and how that data is used.

What Google/YouTube data the app accesses

The app authenticates with a single Google account (the channel owner's own account) and requests the following scopes:

  • youtube.upload — used to upload video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and tags to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • youtube.readonly — used to read the owner's own channel and video metadata, so the app can avoid duplicate uploads and track publishing status.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — used to read the owner's own channel performance data (views, watch time, audience retention) so the app can evaluate past videos and inform future content decisions.

How this data is used

Data obtained through these scopes is used exclusively to operate the app's own core function: producing and managing content for the owner's own YouTube channel. Channel analytics may be used as input when generating suggestions for future video topics, but this data is never used to train, fine-tune, or otherwise build any general-purpose AI or machine-learning model. It is used only as one-time context for individual content-planning requests.

What this data is not used for

  • It is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party.
  • It is never used for advertising or ad targeting.
  • It is never used to train AI/ML models of any kind.
  • It is never accessed on behalf of, or used to profile, any person other than the app's single owner.

Where this data is stored

Data is stored in a private database controlled solely by the app's owner, and is not accessible to any other person. Authentication credentials are stored securely and are used only to allow the app to act on the owner's own channel.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as needed to operate the app. The owner can delete any stored data, and can revoke the app's access to their Google account at any time via Google Account permissions.
