The Margin Report Autopilot

This is an internal automation tool used to produce and publish video content to a single YouTube channel, "The Margin Report." It handles topic research, script writing, voice synthesis, video rendering, quality checks, and uploading, and reads the channel's own analytics to inform future content decisions.

This application is operated by a single individual for their own YouTube channel and is not distributed or offered as a service to other users.
